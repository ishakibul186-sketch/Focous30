Privacy Policy

Your data is used exclusively to generate your personal dashboard and calculate your discipline scores. We do not sell your data to advertisers or third parties.

Focus30 utilizes Google Firebase's industry-standard security protocols to ensure your data is encrypted and protected from unauthorized access.

4. User Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting the administrator.
