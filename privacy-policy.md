# Privacy Policy for Focus30

**Last Updated: October 2023**

Focus30 is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you sign up, we collect your email address through **Firebase Authentication**. This is used to secure your account and allow you to log in from different devices.

### Profile Data
We collect information you provide during profile setup, such as your name, age, and primary goals, to personalize your experience.

### Daily Routine Logs
The core function of Focus30 is to track your daily habits. We store your daily logs (e.g., hygiene, exercise, study hours, sleep) in a **Firebase Realtime Database** to provide you with progress analytics and scoring.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain our service.
- To calculate your daily scores and habit completion percentages.
- To generate the visual progress charts in your Dashboard.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
All data is stored securely using **Google Firebase**. We implement industry-standard security measures provided by Firebase to protect your data from unauthorized access.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use Firebase (a Google service) for authentication and data storage. We do not sell or share your personal data with any other third parties.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to access the data we store about you. Since all data is linked to your account, you can see your history within the application.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer, **Shakibul Islam**.
